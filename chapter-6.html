Chapter 6 — My Final Words

Mere ehsaas ko meri baaton se nahi, mere kirdar se parakhna.

Ab shayad tum samajh rahi hogi ki main itni baatein kyun kar raha hoon.

Isliye nahi ki mujhe tumse koi jawab chahiye. Balki isliye ki kuch ehsaas aise hote hain jinhein dil mein rakhte-rakhte ek waqt ke baad alfaaz dena zaroori ho jaata hai.

Main tumhe pasand karta hoon. Lekin sirf pasand karna hi meri baat ka poora matlab nahi hai.

Main tumhe izzat ke saath jaan-na chahta hoon. Tumhari soch, tumhare khwaab, tumhari pasand-naapasand aur woh sab kuch jo shayad abhi hamari guftagu ka hissa bhi nahi bana.

Aur agar kabhi tum bhi ye mehsoos karo ki hum ek doosre ko samajhne ka mauka de sakte hain, toh main is rishte ko sirf chand achhi baaton tak mehdood nahi rakhna chahta. Main ise sachchai, bharose aur izzat ke saath aage badhana chahta hoon.

Mujhe tumse koi jaldi nahi. Na kisi faisle ka dabaav hai, na kisi waade ki maang.

Bas ek khwahish hai...

Ki jis tarah main tumhari chhoti-chhoti baaton ko ahmiyat deta hoon, kabhi tum bhi mere alfaaz ke peeche chhupe ehsaas ko samajhne ki koshish karo.

Kyunki har shakhs ko zindagi mein koi na koi milta hai... Magar har kisi ko koi aisa nahi milta jo use sirf paane ki nahi, samajhne ki bhi khwahish rakhta ho.

Aur tumse meri bas itni si guzarish hai...

Mere ehsaas ko meri baaton se nahi, mere kirdar se parakhna.

Agar kabhi tumhe lage ki is anjaan mulaqat ko ek khoobsurat rishte mein badalne ka mauka diya ja sakta hai, toh main us safar ke liye sanjeedgi se tayyar hoon.

Baaki, kuch faisle waqt par chhod dena hi behtar hota hai.

Aakhir... Har khoobsurat kahani ka anjaam pehle se likha hua nahi hota. Kuch kahaniyan do log milkar likhte hain.
