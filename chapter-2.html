Chapter 2 — The Little Things

Tumhara kam bolna.

Pata hai, tumhari ek baat mujhe shuru se alag lagi...

Aajkal log apni maujoodgi ka ehsaas dilane ke liye kitne alfaaz kharch kar dete hain, magar tumhari khamoshi mein bhi ek alag si tehzeeb mehsoos hoti thi. Tumhara baat karne ka andaaz, har baat par be-wajah apne alfaaz na lutana, aur apni saadgi mein rehna... Ye sab dheere-dheere tumhari ek aisi tasveer banate gaye, jise main sirf dekhna nahi, samajhna bhi chahta tha.

Mujhe tumhari tarbiyat ki jhalak tumhari chhoti-chhoti baaton mein nazar aayi. Aur shayad kisi ko pasand karne se pehle uske kirdar ki qadar karna hi woh pehli cheez thi jisne mujhe tumhari taraf mutawajjah kiya.
