Chapter 4 — Beyond Words

Tumhara hijab, apni hudood ka khayal, waqt aur jagah ki samajh...

In cheezon ne mere dil mein tumhare liye ek khaas ehtiram paida kiya. Aur shayad isi ehtiram ke darmiyan kab tumhare liye ek khaas ehsaas jagah banne laga, mujhe khud bhi pata nahi chala.

Main tumse ek baat aur kehna chahta hoon.

Shayad tumhare zehan mein ye sawaal aaye ki aakhir main tumhe kitna jaanta hoon? Kya chand guftagu aur kuch tasveeron se kisi insaan ko samjha ja sakta hai?

Nahi, shayad poori tarah nahi.

Lekin kabhi-kabhi insaan kisi ko jaanne ke liye uski har kahani nahi jaanta, bas uski kuch aadatein uske zehan mein reh jaati hain.

Main ek haqeeqi insaan ko samajhna chahta hoon, uski khoobiyon ke saath, uski kamiyon ke saath.
