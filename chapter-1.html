Hamari kahani ka aaghaz bhi toh kuch aisa hi tha...

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon, zindagi kitni ajeeb tareeqe se kuch logon ko humse mila deti hai...

Na koi pehle se pehchaan hoti hai, na mulaqat ki koi khaas wajah. Bas ek chhota sa sabab hota hai, aur uske baad shuru hota hai ek aisa safar, jiske baare mein insaan ne kabhi socha bhi nahi hota.

Hamari kahani ka aaghaz bhi toh kuch aisa hi tha...

Meri cousin ne kisi kaam ke silsile mein tumhe meri ID di thi. Us waqt tum mere liye ek anjaan shakhs thi, aur main tumhare liye sirf ek anjaan naam. Phir dheere-dheere baat hone lagi. Kuch guftagu hui, kuch khamoshiyan rahin, aur inhi ke darmiyan maine tumhe jaan-na shuru kiya.
