Chapter 5 — Something I Hope You Understand

Bas ek khayal hai jo tumhare saamne rakhna chahta hoon...

Maine tumhare kuch aise snaps bhi dekhe hain jinmein tum apne doston ke saath zyada be-fikr aur azaad andaaz mein nazar aati ho. Main ye bhi jaanta hoon ki shayad tum apni zindagi ko mujhse bilkul alag nazariye se dekhti ho.

Main tumhe un tasveeron ki buniyad par judge nahi karna chahta, na tumhe ye mehsoos karwana chahta hoon ki tumhe apni zindagi mere mutabiq jeeni chahiye.

Tumhari saadgi aur tumhara apne kirdar ka khayal rakhna unhi khoobsuratiyon mein se hai. Umeed hai waqt ke saath tum khud bhi unki qadar aur gehraai se mehsoos karogi.

Aur agar meri soch mein kahin koi kami ho, toh main tumhari baat samajhne ke liye bhi tayyar hoon.
