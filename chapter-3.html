Chapter 3 — A Smile to Remember

Phir aati hai tumhari muskurahat...

Na jaane tumhe iska ehsaas bhi hai ya nahi, lekin tumhari muskurati hui tasveeron mein kuch aisa hai jo lafzon mein samjhana mushkil hai. Kabhi dil karta hai ki duniya ki saari pareshaniyan kuch der ke liye wahin ruk jaayein, aur bas woh muskurahat yunhi bani rahe.

Ajeeb baat hai na... Kisi ki ek muskurahat insaan ke andar itna sukoon kaise bhar sakti hai?
